About Us

SUPER STRUCTURE AFRICA,Ltd.

SUPER STRUCTURE AFRICA Ltd is a leading provider of comprehensive civil engineering services, specializing in the planning, design, and construction of infrastructure projects. With decades of experience in the industry, the company is committed to delivering high-quality, sustainable, and cost-effective solutions to meet the growing demands of urban development, transportation, and environmental systems. Super Structure Africa Ltd is a leading provider of premium aluminum fabrication and installation services across Africa. With a reputation built on precision engineering, aesthetic excellence, and unmatched durability, we specialize in transforming architectural visions into reality through expertly crafted windows, doors, and sky curtain wall systems.
